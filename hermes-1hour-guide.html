# Hermes + Claude — 1-Hour Layman Setup Guide **For:** Dr Khairi's USM friend (non-technical) **Goal:** Understand Hermes and Claude, and be able to use Hermes within 1 hour **Prerequisites:** A Mac, an internet connection, ability to type and search. No coding. --- ## 00:00–00:10 — What is Hermes? (the non-technical version) Hermes is your AI **agent** — not just a chatbot. | Chatbot | Hermes | |---------|--------| | Answers one question | Plans your work, hands it to the right bots, checks the answer | | You do all the thinking | You set one goal; Hermes splits it into tasks | | No memory of your work | Specialist bots remember their jobs (café, thesis, repairs, numbers) | | Can post or send on its own | Never posts, spends, or deletes without your OK | **Analogy:** Claude is the brain. Hermes is the manager who uses that brain, delegates to specialist staff, checks their work, and brings you the final decision. **Specialist bots you'll meet:** - **cremabot** — Crema Cafe operations and marketing - **drphbot** — Your DrPH thesis work - **vision** — System repairs and maintenance - **friday** — Numbers and evidence - **llmbot** — Local AI running on your own Mac (private, offline-capable) --- ## 00:10–00:25 — Step 1: Open Hermes on your Mac 1. Open the **Hermes desktop app** on your Mac. - If you don't see it, look in Applications or search Spotlight (Cmd + Space) for "Hermes". 2. **Log in.** Use the account Dr Khairi set up for you. If you don't have credentials, ask him — do not guess or create a new account. 3. You'll see a **chat window**. That's your main workspace. Everything starts here. > 💡 If you're on your phone: Hermes works the same way. Same chat, same bots. --- ## 00:25–00:40 — Step 2: Send your first message Type this exactly (or something like it): > "Hello Hermes. I'm new here. Can you plan my week for me? I teach at USM and I help run a café." **What happens next:** 1. Hermes reads your message. 2. It splits it into tasks (teaching prep, café admin, personal admin). 3. It routes each task to the right model — fast (Haiku) for simple things, deep (Opus) for harder ones. 4. It shows you a plan, not just a reply. **Your job:** Read the plan. Say "yes" or ask for changes. You're the editor. Try a second message: > "Draft an email to a colleague rescheduling next Monday's meeting to Wednesday." Hermes drafts it. You check it. You send it (or not). Hermes **never** sends email as you without your OK. --- ## 00:40–00:50 — Step 3: Choose the right "brain" for the task Hermes lets you pick how hard to think: | Level | Model | Use it for | |-------|-------|------------| | ⚡ Fast | Claude Haiku | Looking things up, summarising, quick edits | | 🧩 Standard | Claude Sonnet (default) | Drafting emails, lesson prep, meeting notes, most daily work | | 🔬 Deep | Claude Opus | Thesis design, statistics, hard decisions, final review | | 🧐 Second opinion | GPT-6 Luna | Critiques important plans (different company's model) | **Rule of thumb:** Start fast. Climb to deep only when the task matters (thesis, big café call). Never use second opinion for captions or casual messages. --- ## 00:50–01:00 — Step 4: Three things you can do today Pick one and try it now: 1. **Draft an email** — "Write an email to my USM colleague about [topic]." 2. **Summarise notes** — Paste rough meeting notes: "Turn these into bullet points and open questions." 3. **Make a to-do list** — "I have a busy Thursday. Turn this mess into a simple to-do list: [paste your mess]." After each one, ask Hermes: "What would you NOT use yourself for?" — this builds your sense of what AI can and can't do. --- ## 01:00 — You're done You now know: - What Hermes is (an agent with a manager + specialist staff) - How to open it and send a message - Which brain to use (fast → standard → deep) - That nothing posts, spends, or deletes without your OK **Next:** Ask Dr Khairi for the passover file (the setup template) if you want to install Hermes yourself, or just keep using the app he's already set up. --- ### Quick-reference glossary | Term | Plain meaning | |------|---------------| | Prompt | The instruction you type to Hermes | | Hallucination | AI making something up confidently — always double-check | | Model | The "brain" behind the answer (Haiku, Sonnet, Opus) | | Bot / specialist | A Hermes worker with one job and its own memory | | Work board | A to-do board where multi-step tasks move from "to do" to "done" | ### Remember - **You decide.** Hermes recommends; you approve. - **Verify important info.** AI can invent facts. - **No jargon needed.** If you can type and search, you can use Hermes. --- *Sedia berkhidmat, doktor. 🫡*
