Hermes

Hermes is your agent, not a chatbot

A chatbot answers one question. Hermes plans your work, hands it to the right bots and models, checks the answer, and brings you a decision.

🧠

Boss bot (JARVIS)

Takes your goal and splits it into tasks. Decides which specialist does what.

👥

Specialist bots

Each has one job and its own memory. Crema for the café, drphbot for your thesis, vision for repairs, friday for numbers.

🔀

Delegation + checks

Quick jobs go to a fast model. Big work goes on a named board. Nothing posts, spends, or deletes without your OK.

Claude

Claude is the brain behind Hermes

Claude (by Anthropic) is the large language model Hermes calls on. It does the thinking — drafting, summarising, reasoning, coding help.

⚡

Fast — Haiku

Lookups, summaries, simple edits. Cheap and quick.

🧩

Standard — Sonnet

Default for drafting, research write-ups, most daily tasks.

🔬

Deep — Opus

Research design, statistics, hard problems, final review. Use when stakes are high.

🧐

Second opinion — GPT-6 Luna

A different company's model critiques Claude's plan. Each model misses different things, so together they catch blind spots. Used for thesis design and big decisions — never for captions.

General AI

General AI in one minute

AI is like a very fast intern who has read almost everything but has no common sense. Useful, needs clear instructions, and must be double-checked.

BeliefReality
AI = search engine✗ It predicts words. It doesn't retrieve truth. It can invent facts.
AI is always right✗ It hallucinates. Always verify important info.
You need to code✓ If you can type and search, you can use AI.
AI replaces you✓ It replaces tasks, not judgment. You're the editor.
